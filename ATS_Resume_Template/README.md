
# ATS-Friendly Resume Template

A collection of professional resume templates for students, freshers and software developers preparing for internships and placements.

## 1. Jake's Resume Template

A clean LaTeX resume template that you can customize for your own requirements.

**Overleaf Link:**  
https://www.overleaf.com/latex/templates/jakes-resume/syzfjbzwjncs

## How to Use

1. Open the Overleaf link.
2. Click "Open as Template".
3. Replace the sample information with your details.
4. Add your education, skills, projects and experience.
5. Compile and download your resume as a PDF.

## Important

Always check your final resume for readability, correct information and ATS compatibility.
